Mom's MSG-free Lettuce Wrap

Description

This is an MSG-free chinese lettuce wrap recipe adapted by Coco for Mom. I switched Soy sauce for Coconut Aminos and Hoisin sauce for Pickapeppa Sauce.

Full Ingredients

  • 1lb Ground Chicken
  • 1 Yellow Onion
  • 1 red or green bell pepper
  • 1 8oz can of water chestnuts
  • 4 tablespoons of Coconut Aminos
  • 2 tablespoons of Pickapeppa
  • 2 tablespoons of rice vinegar
  • 1 tablespoon of sesame oil
  • 1 tablespoon of cornstarch
  • 3 cloves of minced garlic/3 tablespoons of minced garlic
  • An inch of peeled and minced ginger/1/2 teaspoon of dried ginger
  • 2 small heads of bib lettuce

Mis en Place

The Sauce

  1. Gather the ingredients.
    • 4 tablespoons of Coconut Aminos
    • 2 tablespoons of Pickapeppa
    • 2 tablespoons of rice vinegar
    • 1 tablespoon of sesame oil
    • 1 tablespoon of cornstarch
    • 3 cloves of minced garlic/3 tablespoons of minced garlic
    • An inch of peeled and minced ginger/1/2 teaspoon of dried ginger
  2. Combine the ingredients and let sit.

The Cornstarch Slurry

  1. In a small bowl, combine 1 tablespoon of cornstarch to 2 tablespoons of water.
  2. Let sit with a utensil to stir again as needed before pouring the slurry into the saucepan at the end of the recipe.

Chopping

  1. Mince the garlic (if necesssary)
  2. Peel and mince or grate the ginger (if necessary)
  3. Dice the onion
  4. Dice the bell pepper
  5. Dice the water chestnuts
  6. Thinly slice the green onion greens

Stir frying

  1. Heat 1 tsp of oil in a large skillet over medium heat until shimmering.
  2. Spread 1lb of ground chicken in the pan to brown. Leave it alone, do not stir for about 5 minutes until the meat browns on the bottom. Flip the meat to brown the other side and repeat.
  3. When the chicken is browned, remove it from the pan and set it aside in a big bowl
