Deirdre's Rump Roast

Description

This is a delectable rump roast cooked rare with a balsamic dijon vinagrette that makes a delectable jus gravy, caramelized onions, and mushrooms. It is a popular dish in the Provosty and Guillot families and has even caught the eye of world reknown chef Emeril, who once asked Deirdre for the recipe after samping the dish at a friend's engagement party.
