Garlic and Herb Blend

Description

This is a garlic and herb blend based on the Garlic and Herb Good Seasongs salad dressing packets that I can never find in stores. The stores only have the Italian Good Seasongs packets, which are wrong for the Guillot recipes such as Deirdre's Roast

Big Batch Ingredients

For an 8oz jar

  • 36g Garlic Powder
  • 20g Onion Powder
  • 8g Dried Mustard
  • 8g Black Pepper
  • 12g Dried Parsley
  • 12g Dried Basil
  • 4g Dried Oregano
  • 4g Dried Thyme
  • 4g Celery Seed

Single Serving Ingredients in grams

  • 9g Garlic Powder
  • 5g Onion Powder
  • 2g Dried Mustard
  • 2g Black Pepper
  • 3g Dried Parsley
  • 3g Dried Basil
  • 1g Dried Oregano
  • 1g Dried Thyme
  • 1g Celery Seed
